A living harmonic instrument in your macOS menu bar.

Acoustic Circuit taps your Mac's system audio directly via Core Audio Process Taps — zero virtual audio drivers — and renders real-time 120Hz Metal visuals, Circle of Fifths chromagrams, and musical key detection in a 2.3 MB native Swift app.
