## Description

Satie is a native generative focus soundscape instrument and real-time Metal visualizer for iPhone and iPad—inspired by Erik Satie's "furniture music" and Braun industrial minimalism.

**Five Unified Audiovisual Worlds**
- **FOCUS — Soft Light:** Velvet felt piano and soft-mallet vibraphone paired with a note-plucked 3D connectome sculpture (*Circuit*).
- **PULSE — Kinetic Pulse:** Warm analog pulse and hypnotic sub-groove paired with a trigger-locked CRT Lissajous oscilloscope (*Scope*).
- **RAIN — Rain & Glass:** Real courtyard rain with singing bowls and concert harp paired with expanding concentric water ripples (*Ecology*).
- **STILL — Still:** Quiet non-melodic harmonic stasis paired with an audio-coupled monochrome horizon plane (*Horizon*).
- **NIGHT — Gymnopédie:** Slow nocturnal felt piano and cello swells paired with warm candlelight ink blooms (*Nocturne*).

**Built for Real iPhone & iPad Workflows**
- **100% Offline & Private:** Every note is synthesized in real time from bundled CC0 studio acoustic samples and physical modeling. Zero accounts, zero analytics, zero network calls, zero permissions.
- **Background & Lock Screen Playback:** Continues cleanly with the screen locked, with Lock Screen artwork, AirPods / Control Center transport controls, and AirPlay routing.
- **Mix with Other Audio:** Optionally layer Satie (or add Rain / Brown Noise) underneath podcasts, audiobooks, or lectures.
- **Sleep & Focus Timer:** Set 15m, 25m, 45m, 60m, or 90m fade-out timers with a gentle resolving chime.
- **Immersion & Desk Stand Mode:** Tap the canvas to hide all controls, swipe left or right to pair any visualizer with any soundscape, or rotate horizontally with Keep Screen Awake on a desk stand.
